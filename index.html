SeeSharp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SeeSharp ("the app") is developed by Tonay Mete.

Data collection

SeeSharp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts.

Camera

SeeSharp requests the Camera permission to function as a magnifier. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device to display the live magnified view. No image or video is ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored unless you explicitly choose to save it to your device's gallery or share it using Android's share feature.

Local storage

  • The app remembers your last-used zoom level and color filter, stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences), never transmitted anywhere.
  • If you tap "Save," the resulting image is written to your device's Pictures/SeeSharp folder using Android's standard MediaStore, like any other photo.
  • If you tap "Share," a temporary copy of the image is created in the app's private cache to hand off to the app you choose (e.g., Messages, WhatsApp, Email) via Android's share sheet. SeeSharp does not see or control what the receiving app does with that image.

In-app purchase

SeeSharp offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove a small watermark from saved/shared images. This purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing; SeeSharp does not receive or store any payment information.

Permissions

SeeSharp requests only the Camera permission. It does not request location, contacts, storage (beyond the standard scoped access needed to save/share a photo), microphone, or any other permission.

Children's privacy

SeeSharp does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
